The Five Firsts

Five kinds of stone. Five prices.

A woken stone will burn a man, drown him, grind him, hollow him, or make him calm as a tomb. It wakes for no one who goes looking.

Coal

Burns

The forge-mark. Lifts iron from the fire barehanded. The price is the cough that never leaves.

Brine

Drowns

The sea-mark. Sinks like a sounding-lead. The price is the cold that arrives early and stays.

Tor

Grinds

The earth-mark. Carries double a man's load. The price is joints that grind by forty.

Whistler

Hollows

The wind-mark. Half a man's weight, the fastest thing that is not weather. The price is bones that break where a man would bruise.

Gray

Quiets

The death-mark. Calm as a tomb. Reads the last miles on a body. The price is the hush that spreads in the blood.

"You count his soldiers. I count his graves. Count again."

Two Continents · Nine Kingdoms
